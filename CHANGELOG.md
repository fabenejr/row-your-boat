# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Continuous rowing: press the row key to start/stop (optional hold-to-row).
- Thrust matches a full sail in a strong tailwind, per ship type, with a smooth ramp up/down.
- Visible, animated oar (drive, lift and feathered recovery), shown only while rowing and synced to all players.
- Oar model reused from the Karve's steering oar, with a procedural fallback.
- Stamina drain changed to 1 every 10 seconds of rowing.
- Build path moved to a git-ignored `Local.props`.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: per-stroke impulse on key press with cooldown and stamina cost.
